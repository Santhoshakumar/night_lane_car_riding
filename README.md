# night_lane_car_riding
I built a top-down driving game called Night Lane. You steer a car down a four-lane road, pass slower traffic, and try to go as far as you can. I haven’t played it myself, so the handling numbers are untested.

Controls

    ↑ accelerates
    ↓ brakes (your brake lights glow red)
    ← → steer, and the car only turns once it’s moving
    Enter, Space or a tap restarts after a crash

How it plays

    Speed builds gradually, so you have to brake early when you catch up to a slower car.
    Driving onto the grass slows you hard and shakes the screen.
    Hitting another car ends the run, and your best distance is saved in the browser.
    On touch screens, on-screen Go, Brake and steer buttons appear.
